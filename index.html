About SlimeSmash

Retro Pixel Reflex Survival

SlimeSmash is an independent, fast-paced retro pixel arcade wave-survival game built for reflex-driven players. Test your reaction speed across 50+ hand-crafted Adventure levels, customize active and passive pet companion squad loadouts, or test your endurance in Casual Endless mode. Featuring global Google Play leaderboards, daily streak rewards, and native PC keyboard controls via Google Play Games on PC, SlimeSmash delivers a pure skill-based experience with zero pay-to-win mechanics.

What's in the game

Everything you need to smash

Pets & Companions

Unlock passive and active pets. Each companion introduces unique active battle abilities and passive synergy loadouts.

Adventure Mode

50+ levels with increasing difficulty, special modes like Surf Mode, and unique enemy slime types like Devourers.

Casual Mode

Endless waves, hearts, and unique slime effects. Pure score chasing and reflex endurance testing without timers.

Global Leaderboards

Compete worldwide via Google Play integration. Track wave clearance totals and see how high you can rank globally.

Daily Rewards

Earn daily streak bonuses and complete action missions to gather gold coins for unlocks and powerups.

Upgrades & Store

Spend gold coins earned entirely in-game to summon rare pets, upgrade companion stats, and boost your survival power.
