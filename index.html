About SlimeSmash

Retro Pixel Reflex Survival

SlimeSmash is an independent, fast-paced pixel arcade game built for reflex-driven players. Beat the clock across 50+ hand-crafted Adventure levels, survive endless waves, equip pets with game-changing abilities, and climb the global leaderboards.

How it works

Easy to learn, hard to master

  1. 1

    Pop slimes

    Tap fast and chain combos. Every level has a timer and a target score.

  2. 2

    Earn and unlock

    Collect gold coins, then unlock pets and icons in the Store.

  3. 3

    Climb the ranks

    Sign in with Google Play Games to appear on the global leaderboards.
