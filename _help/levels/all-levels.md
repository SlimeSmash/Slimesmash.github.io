Adventure Mode has **50 levels** in five zones of ten. Every 5th level is a [Surf level](/help/modes/surf-mode/). Each level has a target score and a timer; see [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) for the rules.

Values are the launch values and can be tuned remotely between updates.

## Zones

| Zone | Levels | Backdrop | New slimes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 | 1–10 | Sky | Normal, Poison, Money, Extra Time, Explosive, Shield |
| Zone 2 | 11–20 | Desert | Trap |
| Zone 3 | 21–30 | Graveyard | Split |
| Zone 4 | 31–40 | Snow | Mega Combo |
| Zone 5 | 41–50 | Storm | Chain-Reaction |
