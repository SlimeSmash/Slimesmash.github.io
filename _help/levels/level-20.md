Level 20 is a **Surf level** in Zone 2. You need **51 points** before the **59-second** timer runs out, which works out to 0.86 points per second.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 51 |
| Time limit | 59 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 0.92 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 12 |
| Completion reward | 175 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–20) | 2225 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## Slime mix

Each spawn picks one of these slimes at random using these odds:

| Slime | Chance | Points |
|---|---|---|
| [Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) | 58.8% | 1 |
| [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) | 10.6% | 0 (−3 s) |
| [Money](/help/slimes/money-slime/) | 7.1% | 0 (+5 coins) |
| [Extra Time](/help/slimes/extra-time-slime/) | 4.7% | 0 (+2 s) |
| [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) | 5.9% | 3 |
| [Shield](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) | 7.1% | 2 (two taps) |
| [Trap](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) | 5.9% | −1 (halves gains for 2 s) |

## How hard is it?

About **64 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **58 points**, so the target asks for about **88%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Tight**.

There is very little slack. Skip Poison and Trap slimes, and use explosions to catch up when you fall behind.

## Tips

- Slimes enter from the right and slide left at a base speed of 280, with random speed and wave motion. They speed up by up to 35% as the timer runs down. A good slime that leaves the screen costs you **3 seconds** and breaks your combo. See [Surf Mode](/help/modes/surf-mode/).
- Pet passives that depend on slimes expiring, such as [Bones](/help/pets/bones/), do nothing here because surf slimes never time out.
- Poison slimes are bad slimes: tapping one costs 3 seconds. Leave them alone, or use [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Trap slimes cost a point and halve your score gains for 2 seconds. Do not tap them right before a chain.
- Money slimes score nothing but add 5 coins each to the level total, paid when you win.
- Always tap Extra Time slimes. Two seconds is about two and a half extra spawns here.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **175 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 21.
- Achievement: **Slime Crusher** (Google Play Games).
