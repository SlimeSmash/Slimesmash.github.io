Level 3 is a standard level in Zone 1. You need **17 points** before the **40-second** timer runs out, which works out to 0.42 points per second.

**New here:** [Money Slime](/help/slimes/money-slime/) appears for the first time. The Slime Discovery panel opens the first time you meet it.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 17 |
| Time limit | 40 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 0.99 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 8 |
| Completion reward | 66 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–3) | 181 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## Slime mix

Each spawn picks one of these slimes at random using these odds:

| Slime | Chance | Points |
|---|---|---|
| [Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) | 76.9% | 1 |
| [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) | 13.8% | 0 (−3 s) |
| [Money](/help/slimes/money-slime/) | 9.2% | 0 (+5 coins) |

## How hard is it?

About **40 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **31 points**, so the target asks for about **55%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Relaxed**.

You can afford to miss some slimes, so use the spare time to practice avoiding bad slimes.

## Tips

- Slimes live for 4 to 5 seconds. A missed good slime only wastes the chance in Adventure Mode, so keep your taps on the slimes closest to expiring.
- Poison slimes are bad slimes: tapping one costs 3 seconds. Leave them alone, or use [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Money slimes score nothing but add 5 coins each to the level total, paid when you win.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **66 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 4 and unlocks the pet **[Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/)**.
