Level 35 is a **Surf level** in Zone 4. You need **81 points** before the **70-second** timer runs out, which works out to 1.16 points per second.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 81 |
| Time limit | 70 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 0.86 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 16 |
| Completion reward | 280 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–35) | 5634 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## How hard is it?

About **81 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **85 points**, so the target asks for about **95%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Tight**.

There is very little slack. Skip Poison and Trap slimes, and use explosions to catch up when you fall behind.

## Tips

- Slimes enter from the right and slide left at a base speed of 310, with random speed and wave motion. They speed up by up to 35% as the timer runs down. A good slime that leaves the screen costs you **3 seconds** and breaks your combo. See [Surf Mode](/help/modes/surf-mode/).
- Pet passives that depend on slimes expiring, such as [Bones](/help/pets/bones/), do nothing here because surf slimes never time out.
- Poison slimes are bad slimes: tapping one costs 3 seconds. Leave them alone, or use [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Trap slimes cost a point and halve your score gains for 2 seconds. Do not tap them right before a chain.
- Money slimes score nothing but add 5 coins each to the level total, paid when you win.
- Always tap Extra Time slimes. Two seconds is about two and a half extra spawns here.
- Pop a Mega Combo slime when many slimes are on screen so the ×2 window lasts through several taps.
- A Split slime is worth four slimes. Pop it as early as possible so the new slimes have time to be tapped.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **280 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 36.
- Achievement: **Veteran** (Google Play Games).
