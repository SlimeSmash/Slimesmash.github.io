Level 18 is a standard level in Zone 2. You need **47 points** before the **58-second** timer runs out, which works out to 0.81 points per second.

**New here:** [Trap Slime](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) appears for the first time. The Slime Discovery panel opens the first time you meet it.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 47 |
| Time limit | 58 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 0.93 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 12 |
| Completion reward | 150 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–18) | 1879 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## How hard is it?

About **62 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **56 points**, so the target asks for about **83%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Standard**.

You can afford to miss some slimes, so use the spare time to practice avoiding bad slimes.

## Tips

- Slimes live for 4 to 5 seconds. A missed good slime only wastes the chance in Adventure Mode, so keep your taps on the slimes closest to expiring.
- Poison slimes are bad slimes: tapping one costs 3 seconds. Leave them alone, or use [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Trap slimes cost a point and halve your score gains for 2 seconds. Do not tap them right before a chain.
- Money slimes score nothing but add 5 coins each to the level total, paid when you win.
- Always tap Extra Time slimes. Two seconds is about two and a half extra spawns here.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **150 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 19.
