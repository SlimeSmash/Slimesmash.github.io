Level 9 is a standard level in Zone 1. You need **29 points** before the **51-second** timer runs out, which works out to 0.57 points per second.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 29 |
| Time limit | 51 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 0.97 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 10 |
| Completion reward | 98 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–9) | 702 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## Slime mix

Each spawn picks one of these slimes at random using these odds:

| Slime | Chance | Points |
|---|---|---|
| [Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) | 67.6% | 1 |
| [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) | 12.2% | 0 (−3 s) |
| [Money](/help/slimes/money-slime/) | 8.1% | 0 (+5 coins) |
| [Extra Time](/help/slimes/extra-time-slime/) | 5.4% | 0 (+2 s) |
| [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) | 6.8% | 3 |

## How hard is it?

About **53 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **46 points**, so the target asks for about **63%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Standard**.

You can afford to miss some slimes, so use the spare time to practice avoiding bad slimes.

## Tips

- Slimes live for 4 to 5 seconds. A missed good slime only wastes the chance in Adventure Mode, so keep your taps on the slimes closest to expiring.
- Poison slimes are bad slimes: tapping one costs 3 seconds. Leave them alone, or use [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Money slimes score nothing but add 5 coins each to the level total, paid when you win.
- Always tap Extra Time slimes. Two seconds is about two and a half extra spawns here.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **98 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 10.
