Level 1 is a standard level in Zone 1. You need **14 points** before the **35-second** timer runs out, which works out to 0.40 points per second.

**New here:** [Normal Slime](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) appears for the first time. The Slime Discovery panel opens the first time you meet it.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 14 |
| Time limit | 35 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 1 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 8 |
| Completion reward | 49 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–1) | 49 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## Slime mix

Each spawn picks one of these slimes at random using these odds:

| Slime | Chance | Points |
|---|---|---|
| [Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) | 100.0% | 1 |

## How hard is it?

About **35 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **35 points**, so the target asks for about **40%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Relaxed**.

You can afford to miss some slimes, so use the spare time to practice avoiding bad slimes.

## Tips

- Slimes live for 4 to 5 seconds. A missed good slime only wastes the chance in Adventure Mode, so keep your taps on the slimes closest to expiring.
- This level runs the tutorial: tap green slimes, then try your pet button.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **49 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 2.
- Achievement: **First Steps** (Google Play Games).
