Level 5 is a **Surf level** in Zone 1. You need **21 points** before the **48-second** timer runs out, which works out to 0.44 points per second.

**New here:** [Explosive Slime](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) appears for the first time. The Slime Discovery panel opens the first time you meet it.

## Level rules

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Target score | 21 |
| Time limit | 48 s |
| Spawn interval | one slime every 0.98 s (if there is room) |
| Max slimes on screen | 9 |
| Completion reward | 70 coins |
| Coins earned so far (levels 1–5) | 328 |

Values shown are the launch values. Some of them can be tuned between app updates, so your game may differ slightly.

## Slime mix

Each spawn picks one of these slimes at random using these odds:

| Slime | Chance | Points |
|---|---|---|
| [Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) | 67.6% | 1 |
| [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) | 12.2% | 0 (−3 s) |
| [Money](/help/slimes/money-slime/) | 8.1% | 0 (+5 coins) |
| [Extra Time](/help/slimes/extra-time-slime/) | 5.4% | 0 (+2 s) |
| [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) | 6.8% | 3 |

## How hard is it?

About **49 slimes** spawn over the full timer. Popping every good slime once would give roughly **43 points**, so the target asks for about **49%** of that. Difficulty rating: **Relaxed**.

You can afford to miss some slimes, so use the spare time to practice avoiding bad slimes.

## Tips

- Slimes enter from the right and slide left at a base speed of 250, with random speed and wave motion. They speed up by up to 35% as the timer runs down. A good slime that leaves the screen costs you **3 seconds** and breaks your combo. See [Surf Mode](/help/modes/surf-mode/).
- Pet passives that depend on slimes expiring, such as [Bones](/help/pets/bones/), do nothing here because surf slimes never time out.
- Poison slimes are bad slimes: tapping one costs 3 seconds. Leave them alone, or use [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Money slimes score nothing but add 5 coins each to the level total, paid when you win.
- Always tap Extra Time slimes. Two seconds is about two and a half extra spawns here.

## Rewards and unlocks

- Winning pays **70 coins**, plus any coins collected from Money slimes during the run.
- Clearing this level opens level 6.
- Achievement: **Getting the Hang** (Google Play Games).
