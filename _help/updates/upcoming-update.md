## New

- **[Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/)**, a new 360° base defense mode.
- **New loading screen** with a progress bar that shows what the game is doing, such as checking your cloud save.
- **New blue main menu background.**
- **Pet beams.** When a pet ability affects slimes, a beam is drawn from the pet to each one.
- **Player ID** is shown in Settings.
- **Daily Reward button** moved to the main menu.
- **Casual Mode lock screen** shows a progress bar towards unlocking it.
- **Redesigned Adventure Mode and Mode Select screens.**
- **Cleaner HUD and toast messages** that no longer get in the way of play.

## Changes

- **[Surf Mode](/help/modes/surf-mode/)** is faster and speeds up as time runs out.
- **Music removed.** The game has sound effects only, and the Music On/Off panel is gone.
- **Missions:** the daily mission streak is removed. The daily reward streak stays.
- **Stars removed.** Stars were earned for completing levels with complex requirements.
- **The border system is removed.**
- **Splash text removed** from the main menu.
- **Pointless slimes removed.** Slimes that did nothing, or only took 1 score away, are gone.
- **[Devourer](/help/slimes/devourer/) removed.**
- **Companions removed** (this includes [Maximus](/help/pets/maximus/)). [Pets](/help/pets/companions-and-pets/) stay.
- **Pet upgrades removed for now.** Pets will keep improving in small patches after the update (2.0.1 and on).

## Fixes

- Switching your Google Play Games profile no longer creates a duplicate identity.
- Level 1 no longer shows the welcome panel when replayed.
- Corrected slime descriptions.
- Mission text now reads "Kill X slimes" correctly.
- The "level unlocked" notification in wave mode works properly.
- The Surf Mode background and button icon now match.
- A non-fatal crash found through crash reports is fixed.

## Planned, not final

- **A 2.0 look.** A new original SlimeSmash slime with new idle and die animations, redesigned slime variants, a new icon and store graphics, and a new trailer.
- **Mission rework** with more depth and more rewards.
