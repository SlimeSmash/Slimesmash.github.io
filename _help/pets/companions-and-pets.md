Pets change how a level plays. You equip **one pet at a time**, and it works for the whole level.

## Two kinds of pet

- **Passive pets** work all the time with no button to press. Example: [Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/).
- **Ability pets** have a button (or the <kbd>Space</kbd> key on PC). Each ability has a **cooldown** and a **limited number of uses per game**. Both reset at the start of each game. The cooldown pauses while the game is paused. Example: [Frost](/help/pets/frost/).

Some levels turn pets off.

## Getting pets

- **Starter pet:** [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) is free.
- **Level rewards:** some pets unlock automatically when you reach an Adventure level. If you have no pet equipped, the newest one you earn is equipped for you.
- **Store:** the rest are bought in the [Store](/help/guides/coins-and-store/).

Equip and unequip pets from the pet's details panel in your inventory.

## Companions

Companions such as [Maximus](/help/pets/maximus/) are a separate, older system. They are being retired.

## Upgrades

Pets can be upgraded with gold coins, and upgrade progress is shown as a percentage. The upgrade system is being removed for now in the next update.
