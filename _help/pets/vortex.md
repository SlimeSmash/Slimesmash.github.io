Vortex's ability is an emergency button. Press it and **every slime on screen is cleared** instantly.

## How it works

- Cleared slimes do not give points and do not trigger penalties, whether good or bad.
- Two uses per Adventure level, with a 20-second cooldown.
- It is the most expensive pet in the Store.

## When to use it

- In [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/) and [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/), when a wave overwhelms you and hearts are at stake.
- On Adventure levels, to remove a screen full of bad slimes during a crunch.

## Tips

- Pair with [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) in endless modes by switching pets between runs.

## In Casual and Defense Mode

Endless modes have no use limit. Instead the cooldown is lengthened: **base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number). For this pet that is **29 seconds** between uses. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
