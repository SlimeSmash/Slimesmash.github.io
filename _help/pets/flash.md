Flash has the **Overkill** passive. Pop a slime within **0.3 seconds** of it appearing and you earn **5 coins**.

## How it works

- The coin bonus is added to the level's coin total and paid out when you win an Adventure level.
- Only very fast reactions count. On a busy screen, tap new slimes as they appear.

## Tips

- It is a coin-farming pet. On early levels (low goals) you can aim at new slimes to build coins.
- Pair with Money Slimes for extra coins per level.
