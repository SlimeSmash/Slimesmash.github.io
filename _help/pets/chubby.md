Chubby is the first pet every player has. Its ability, **Big Target**, scales slimes up to **150%** size for about **5 seconds**, which also makes them easier to tap.

## How it works

- Press the pet button (or <kbd>Space</kbd> on PC). Every slime on screen grows, and slimes that spawn during the effect grow as well.
- The slimes shrink back smoothly when it ends.
- Three uses per level in Adventure Mode, with a 10-second cooldown that pauses while the game is paused.

## When to use it

- **Shield Slimes:** two taps are easier on a big target.
- **Surf levels:** fast, wavy slimes are easier to catch when they are larger.
- **Ghost Slimes:** a bigger target gives you more room when the slime briefly turns solid.

## Tips

- Hold the first use for a crowded screen rather than opening a level with it.
- Chubby is a perfect pair with [Echo](/help/pets/echo/) because the second pop lands more reliably.

Existing players received Chubby automatically in a past update. New players can choose a first pet during the intro tutorial.
## In Casual and Defense Mode

Endless modes have no use limit. Instead the cooldown is lengthened: **base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number). For this pet that is **16 seconds** between uses. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
