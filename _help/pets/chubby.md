Chubby is the free starter pet. Its ability, **Big Target**, grows every slime on screen to 150% of its size for a few seconds, which makes them easier to hit. A beam is drawn from the pet to each slime it affects.

Added in v1.1.7, The Chubby Update. Players who already had the game received Chubby automatically on their next level.
