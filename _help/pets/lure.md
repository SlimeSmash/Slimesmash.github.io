Lure is a passive pet. After every tap, slimes slowly drift toward the place you tapped, at a speed of **60 pixels per second**.

## How it works

- Works in every mode, with no button and no cooldown.
- It bunches slimes together, which sets up [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/), [Chain-Reaction](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/) and [Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/) blasts.
- It is a gentle pull, not a hard snap. Do not expect slimes to collide with your finger.

## Tips

- Tap the middle of the screen to keep slimes compact.
- On Surf levels, the pull helps with the vertical wave motion.
