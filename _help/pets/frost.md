Frost's ability, **Freeze**, stops every slime in place for **3 seconds**.

## How it works

- While frozen, the level timer does not tick, spawning stops and slime lifetimes pause.
- **Taps on slimes are ignored during the freeze.** Frost is a pause, not a free-pop window.
- Use it to escape a dangerous moment, to let a cooldown finish, or to wait out a bad effect.
- Three uses per Adventure level; cooldown 12 seconds.

## When it shines

- At the end of a level when you are a few points short and the clock is about to expire. It does not add time, but it stops the loss.
- In [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/), when a wave reaches your base and you need space.
- To think. Surf levels are fast, and a 3-second pause helps you read the screen.

## Tips

- Frost does not help against a [Poison Slime](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) already tapped.
- The retired [Freeze Slime](/help/slimes/freeze-slime/) did something similar.

## In Casual and Defense Mode

Endless modes have no use limit. Instead the cooldown is lengthened: **base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number). For this pet that is **18 seconds** between uses. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
