You can only equip one pet at a time, so the best pet depends on what you are playing. This is a suggested order.

## Free pets first

1. **[Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/)** is yours from the start. Use it on early levels.
2. **[Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/)** unlocks at level 4. Equip it and keep it until the mid game.
3. **[Guard](/help/pets/guard/)** unlocks at level 12 and is the best defensive pet in the game.

## First Store purchases

- **[Frost](/help/pets/frost/) or [Echo](/help/pets/echo/) (800 coins).** Frost is better for survival; Echo is better for score.
- **[Bones](/help/pets/bones/) (1,200)** if you play Casual Mode a lot. Skip it for Surf levels.

## Saving up

- **[Zap](/help/pets/zap/) (1,500)** is the best catch-up pet for tight late levels.
- **[Flash](/help/pets/flash/) (1,500)** is only worth it for coin farming.
- **[Vortex](/help/pets/vortex/) (2,000)** is for endless modes.

## By goal

| Goal | Pet |
|---|---|
| Clear a tough Adventure level | Zap, Purify, Rumble |
| Survive on a Surf level | Guard |
| Higher Casual wave | Guard, Vortex, Bones |
| Higher Defense wave | Guard, Frost, Vortex |
| More coins | Flash |
