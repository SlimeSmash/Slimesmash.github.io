Purify's ability, **Swap**, cleans up the screen. For **5 seconds**, bad slimes ([Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) and [Trap](/help/slimes/trap-slime/)) become Normal slimes worth **2 points** each.

## How it works

- Slimes that were already on screen are converted when you activate it.
- Bad slimes that spawn during the 5 seconds are converted too.
- It is a way to turn dangerous waves into points.
- Cooldown 15 seconds, three uses per Adventure level.

## Tips

- Save it for levels with high Poison and Trap odds (the late zones).
- Use before a [Mega Combo](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/) window for even more points.

## In Casual and Defense Mode

Endless modes have no use limit. Instead the cooldown is lengthened: **base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number). For this pet that is **21 seconds** between uses. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
