Zap is a passive pet with the **Auto Click** passive. Periodically it has a **20% chance** to pop a slime for you.

## How it works

- It pops a good slime. It does not target bad slimes.
- No button, no cooldown, no limit.
- Because it plays for you, Zap is a great pet for catching up on high targets.

## Tips

- Zap rewards busy screens, where there are always good slimes to hit.
- Equip it for the late Adventure levels where goals are tight.
