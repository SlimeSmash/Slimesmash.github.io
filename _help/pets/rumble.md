Rumble is a passive pet with the **Aftershock** effect. Every **5th good slime** you pop causes an explosion around the slime, the same size as an [Explosive Slime](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) blast.

## How it works

- Only good slimes count toward the 5. Tapping a bad slime does not add to the counter.
- Slimes popped by the explosion score as usual.
- The blast does not distinguish good from bad slimes. A Poison or Trap slime nearby is popped and its penalty applies.
- No button, no cooldown, no use limit. It works in every mode.

## Tips

- Count your pops. Aim the 5th tap at the middle of a cluster.
- Rumble is the best early pet for steady score on levels 4 to 25.
- Do not stack it mentally with Explosive Slimes: use both and let the blasts overlap.
