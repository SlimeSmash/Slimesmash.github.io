Bones has the **Gravedigger** passive. Whenever a good slime reaches the end of its life without being popped, there is a **30% chance** it comes back, resets its timer and shows "SAVED!".

## How it works

- It only works on slimes that *time out*. Slimes that leave the screen on Surf levels never time out, so Bones does nothing there.
- In Defense Mode slimes do not expire (they march to the base), so Bones does nothing there either.
- Best in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) standard levels and [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/), where an expired slime costs a heart.

## Tips

- Bones is a safety net, not a strategy. It rescues about one in three misses.
