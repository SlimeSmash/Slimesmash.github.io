Guard's ability raises a protective shield for **5 seconds**.

## How it works

- While active, penalties are blocked: the 3 seconds a [Poison Slime](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) takes, the 3 seconds a Surf miss costs, lost hearts in Casual Mode, and hits to your base in Defense Mode.
- It does **not** stop the half-score effect of a [Trap Slime](/help/slimes/trap-slime/).
- Cooldown 10 seconds, three uses per Adventure level.

## When to use it

- On Surf levels, where escaping slimes cost 3 seconds each.
- In Defense Mode, as a wave arrives at the base.
- To tap Poison Slimes deliberately when they sit inside a good cluster.

## Tips

- Guard is a Common pet but one of the strongest for survival in endless modes.

## In Casual and Defense Mode

Endless modes have no use limit. Instead the cooldown is lengthened: **base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number). For this pet that is **16 seconds** between uses. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
