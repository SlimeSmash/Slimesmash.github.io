Echo's ability, **Mirror Pop**, arms your next pop. The next normal slime you pop also pops the slime nearest to it. One tap, two slimes.

- It stays armed for 5 seconds, then wears off if you have not used it.
- A bad slime does not trigger it.
- Works best when slimes are tightly clustered.
