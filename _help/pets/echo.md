Echo's ability, **Mirror Pop**, arms your next tap for 5 seconds. When you pop a good slime while it is armed, the **nearest other slime** pops too.

## How it works

- A bad slime does not trigger the echo. Only good slimes you tap do.
- The echo goes to whichever slime is closest, **even a bad one**, and bad-slime penalties apply.
- Four uses per Adventure level with an 8-second cooldown, the fastest of the ability pets.

## Tips

- Trigger it in dense areas away from Poison and Trap slimes.
- Combine with [Shield Slimes](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) for fast double hits.
- Best used in the quiet moments before a wave of good slimes.

## In Casual and Defense Mode

Endless modes have no use limit. Instead the cooldown is lengthened: **base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number). For this pet that is **12 seconds** between uses. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
