Adventure Mode is the level-based mode. Every level gives you a **time limit** and a **target score**. Pop slimes to score, and have the target reached when the clock hits zero.

## How a level works

1. A 3, 2, 1, GO! countdown starts the level.
2. Slimes appear at random spots. Each level has its own mix of slime types, spawn speed and limit on how many can be on screen at once.
3. Pop slimes to score. Your score turns **green** once you reach the target.
4. The level always runs until the timer reaches zero. **If your score is at or above the target then, you win.** If not, you can retry.

The Win and Fail panels show your score against the target.

## Score, combos and time

- **Combos.** Popping slimes in quick succession builds a combo. It resets if you pause for about a second and a half.
- **Score effects.** [Mega Combo](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/) slimes boost your score for a short time, and [Trap](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) slimes reduce it.
- **Time effects.** Some slimes can add time or take time away. A [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) shield stops you losing time.

See [Slime types](/help/slimes/slime-types/) for the full roster.

## Coins

- You earn coins from the level itself and a completion reward.
- **The completion reward is paid once per level.** Replaying a level shows "Already Claimed!" for that part, but coins you pick up during the run still count.
- Spend coins in the [Store](/help/guides/coins-and-store/).

## Pets

Your equipped [pet](/help/pets/companions-and-pets/) works in most levels. A few levels switch pets off, and then the pet button is hidden. Ability pets show a button with uses left (for example 0/3). On PC, press <kbd>Space</kbd>.

## Progress

- Winning a level saves your progress and opens the next one.
- Reaching level milestones earns [achievements](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/).
- Winning the level set by the lock screen unlocks [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/).
- Some levels are [Surf levels](/help/modes/surf-mode/).
- You can report a problem level from inside the game.
