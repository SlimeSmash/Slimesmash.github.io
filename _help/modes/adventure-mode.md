Adventure Mode is the heart of SlimeSmash. Each level gives you a **target score** and a **time limit**. Pop enough good slimes before the clock runs out to win.

## How a level works

1. A 3-2-1 countdown starts the level (the very first tutorial level skips it).
2. Slimes appear at random places. Each type has a lifetime of 3 to 5 seconds.
3. Tap good slimes to score. Avoid bad slimes.
4. Reach the target score to win. If the timer hits zero first, you lose and can retry.

Backgrounding the app pauses the game automatically.

## The 50 levels

Levels come in five zones of ten, with a new backdrop each zone: sky, desert, graveyard, snow and storm. Every 5th level is a [Surf level](/help/modes/surf-mode/).

| Level | Target | Time | Slimes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1](/help/levels/level-1/) | 14 | 35 s | Normal only |
| [10](/help/levels/level-10/) | 31 | 52 s | Six types, adds Shield |
| [25](/help/levels/level-25/) | 61 | 62 s | Seven types including Trap |
| [50](/help/levels/level-50/) | 113 | 80 s | All ten types |

Browse every level on [All levels](/help/levels/all-levels/).

## What changes as you progress

- Targets rise from 14 points on level 1 to 113 on level 50.
- Slimes spawn faster (one per second on level 1, one per 0.8 s on level 50), and the on-screen cap grows from 8 to 20.
- New slime types are introduced one at a time: [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) (level 2), [Money](/help/slimes/money-slime/) (3), [Extra Time](/help/slimes/extra-time-slime/) (4), [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) (5), [Shield](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) (10), [Trap](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) (18), [Split](/help/slimes/split-slime/) (26), [Mega Combo](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/) (34), [Chain-Reaction](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/) (42).
- From around level 26 the target is higher than simple popping provides, so you must use bonuses.

## Scoring and coins

- Score comes from slime base points. See [Score and multipliers](/help/reference/score-and-multipliers/).
- The combo counter is a visual meter. It shows milestones at 5, 10, 20, 30 and 50 but does not change your score.
- Winning pays the level's completion reward plus 5 coins per [Money Slime](/help/slimes/money-slime/) popped. Failing pays nothing.
- All 50 levels pay **10,752 coins** in completion rewards in total.

## Unlocks

- Clearing levels 3, 11, 15 and 29 unlocks the pets [Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/), [Guard](/help/pets/guard/), [Lure](/help/pets/lure/) and [Purify](/help/pets/purify/).
- Clearing level 4 unlocks [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/).
- Six levels (1, 5, 10, 20, 35, 50) trigger [achievements](/help/reference/achievements/).
