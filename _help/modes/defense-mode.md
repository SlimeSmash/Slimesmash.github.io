In Defense Mode you protect a **base in the middle of the screen**. Slimes spawn from **every direction** (360°) and march straight at it at a steady speed. Pop them before they arrive.

## The base

- The base starts with a **shield**.
- The first slime to reach the base **breaks the shield**. The screen flashes red.
- After a hit, the base is **invulnerable for 1.5 seconds**, so one cluster cannot finish you off.
- The next hit **destroys the base and ends the run**.
- Any slime that reaches the base counts as a hit. Slimes do not expire in this mode, so every slime must be popped.
- A [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) shield blocks base hits while it is active.

## Waves

- Wave 1 sends **6 slimes**. Each next wave adds **2 more**.
- The delay between spawns starts at **1.2 s** and shrinks by 0.02 s per wave, to a minimum of **0.25 s**.
- **Every 5th wave is a Surge:** 1.5 times as many slimes at twice the spawn speed. The wave label turns red ("Wave 10 - SURGE!").
- Clear every slime to finish a wave. A progress bar fills as you deal with each one.
- The background colour changes every 5 waves.

Full numbers are in the [Defense wave table](/help/reference/defense-wave-table/).

## Slimes in Defense Mode

[Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) 40%, [Shield](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) 18%, [Ghost](/help/slimes/ghost-slime/) 15%, [Chain-Reaction](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/) 10%, [Money](/help/slimes/money-slime/) 10%, [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) 7%.

## Checkpoints

- **Every 5 waves** you reach a checkpoint ("Starting from Wave 6").
- **Your next run starts from your last checkpoint**, not from wave 1.

## Rewards and records

- **15 coins** for every wave you complete.
- Beating your best wave shows **NEW RECORD!** and gives a **100-coin bonus**.
- Coins are paid when the run ends.
- Your best wave goes to the Defense leaderboard when you are signed in.

On PC, use <kbd>Esc</kbd> to pause.

## Tips

- Chain-Reaction and Explosive slimes are your best friends: slimes bunch up as they converge on the base.
- Deal with Shield and Ghost slimes first, because they take longest.
- [Frost](/help/pets/frost/) and [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) are the best pets here. See [Defense strategy](/help/guides/defense-strategy/).
