In Defense Mode you protect a **base in the middle of the screen**. Slimes spawn from **every direction** (360°) and march straight at it. Pop them before they arrive.

## The base

- The base is protected by a **shield**.
- The first slime to reach the base **breaks the shield**. The screen flashes red.
- After a hit, the base is **invulnerable for a moment**, so one cluster cannot finish you off.
- The next hit that lands **destroys the base and ends the run**.
- So you can take **one hit**, and the second ends it.

Any slime that reaches the base counts as a hit, including slimes you would normally avoid popping.

## Waves

- Each wave sends **more slimes** than the last, and they arrive **faster**.
- **Clear every slime** to finish a wave. There is a short break before the next.
- A **progress bar** fills as you deal with each wave's slimes.
- **Every 5th wave is a Surge.** It has more slimes that spawn faster, and the wave label turns red ("Wave 10 - SURGE!").
- The **background colour changes every 5 waves**.

## Checkpoints

- **Every 5 waves**, you reach a **checkpoint** ("Starting from Wave 6").
- **Your next run starts from your last checkpoint**, not from wave 1.

## Rewards and records

- You earn **coins for every wave you complete**.
- Beating your best wave shows **NEW RECORD!** and gives a **bonus of 100 coins**.
- Coins are paid when the run ends.
- Your best wave goes to the Defense leaderboard on Google Play Games when you are signed in.

On PC, use <kbd>Esc</kbd> to pause.
