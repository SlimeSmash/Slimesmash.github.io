Surf Mode is a special kind of level inside [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/). It plays like any Adventure level (timer, target score, pets), with one big difference.

## What is different

Normal levels spawn slimes at random spots on the screen. In a Surf level, slimes **enter from the right edge and slide across** at a set speed, each at a random height. You have to catch them on the move.

## Next update

Surf Mode was too slow, so it is being sped up. It now **gets faster as the time left goes down**. A mismatch between the Surf background and its button icon is also fixed. See [Upcoming update](/help/updates/upcoming-update/).

Stability was improved in v1.1.5.
