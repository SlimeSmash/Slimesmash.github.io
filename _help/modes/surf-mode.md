Surf Mode is a special kind of level inside [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/). It plays like any Adventure level (timer, target score, pets) with one big difference.

## What is different

- Slimes **enter from the right edge and slide left**, each at a random height with a gentle wave motion.
- Slimes **never expire**. Instead, a good slime that leaves the left edge is a **miss**: it costs **3 seconds** and breaks your combo. Bad slimes can leave safely.
- Slimes **speed up as time runs out**, by up to 35%.
- Surf levels have their own ocean backdrop.
- Each level has its own base speed, and speed also scales with your screen width so it feels the same on every device.

## Surf levels

Levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50. See [All levels](/help/levels/all-levels/) for each level's target and speed.

## Tips

- Aim ahead of the slime, not at it.
- [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) blocks the 3-second miss penalty. [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) makes targets bigger. [Lure](/help/pets/lure/) helps with wave motion.
- [Bones](/help/pets/bones/) does nothing here, since slimes never time out.
- See [Surf strategy](/help/guides/surf-strategy/).

## History

Surf Mode was redesigned in 2.0. Slimes now move like a real wave, with rotation, and speed up as the time left goes down. A mismatch between the Surf background and its button icon is also fixed. See [What's new in 2.0](/help/updates/whats-new-in-2-0/). Stability was improved in v1.1.5.
