Casual Mode is endless. There is no timer and no target score. You survive wave after wave with **3 hearts**, and your result is the **wave you reach**.

(In the game files it is called Wave Mode, and the unlock message says "Waves Mode".)

## Unlocking

Casual Mode unlocks as you progress through [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/). The lock screen tells you what is needed. A "New Mode Unlocked!" message appears when you get it.

## How a wave works

- Each wave sends **more slimes** than the last, and they come **faster** as the waves go on.
- **You must clear every slime** on screen before the next wave starts.
- There is a short 1.5-second break between waves.
- You earn coins for every wave you clear.

## Hearts

- You start with **3 hearts**. Mistakes cost a heart, and the run ends at 0.
- After losing a heart you are **invincible for 1.5 seconds** while the hearts flash.
- On your last heart it pulses red.
- Some slimes can **heal a heart**.

## Slime effects

Some slimes change the screen for a few seconds:

- **Mirror:** the slime area is flipped sideways for about 5 seconds.
- **Blind:** a dark flash covers the screen for a moment. Your taps still work.
- **Slow-down:** the action freezes briefly (about 2 seconds).

Hitting one of these again while it is active extends it.

## Records and rewards

- Beating your best wave shows **NEW RECORD!** and gives a **bonus of 100 coins**.
- Your best wave is saved and sent to the Casual leaderboard. See [Leaderboards](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/).
- A wave road at the top of the screen shows your progress. Your best wave pulses.

You can pause mid-run (<kbd>Esc</kbd> on PC), and use your pet. On PC, <kbd>Space</kbd> triggers its ability.
