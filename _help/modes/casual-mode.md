Casual Mode is an endless wave survival mode with no timer and no target score. You clear each wave of slimes. Let a good slime expire and you **lose a heart**. Lose all three and the run ends.

## Unlocking

Casual Mode unlocks when you reach **Adventure level 5** (clear level 4). The lock screen shows a progress bar toward it.

## Waves

- Wave 1 has **4 slimes**. Each next wave adds **2 more** (wave 10 has 22).
- Slimes spawn every **0.5 seconds** at first. The delay shrinks by 0.01 s per wave, down to a minimum of **0.15 s** at wave 36.
- You must pop every slime to clear the wave. A 1.5-second break follows.
- Every slime lives 5 seconds. Anything good that expires costs a heart.
- After a heart is lost, you are invulnerable for 1.5 seconds.
- The wave road on screen shows 30 waves, and the game continues past them.

Full numbers are in the [wave table](/help/reference/casual-wave-table/).

## Slimes in Casual Mode

[Normal](/help/slimes/normal-slime/) 50%, [Shield](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) 15%, [Ghost](/help/slimes/ghost-slime/) 10%, [Mirror](/help/slimes/mirror-slime/) 8%, [Blind](/help/slimes/blind-slime/) 7%, [Slow-Mo](/help/slimes/slow-mo-slime/) 7%, [Heart](/help/slimes/heart-slime/) 3%. There are no bad slimes. The annoying ones (Mirror, Blind, Ghost) mess with your view instead.

## Rewards and records

- **10 coins** for every wave you complete.
- Beating your best wave shows **NEW RECORD!** and gives a **100-coin bonus**.
- Coins are paid when the run ends.
- Your best wave is sent to the online leaderboard when you are signed in. See [Leaderboards](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/).

## Pets

Pets work here, but ability pets have **no use limit**. Their cooldowns are longer instead. See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).

## Tips

- Keep the screen small: pop old slimes first.
- Equip [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) or [Vortex](/help/pets/vortex/) for the high waves.
- Prioritise [Heart Slimes](/help/slimes/heart-slime/) when you are on one heart.
