The Money Slime scores nothing, but each one you pop adds **5 coins** to the coins collected during the level.

## How it works

- In [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/), the coins are added to your level total and paid out **when you win** the level. If you fail, they are not paid.
- The win panel shows completion coins and bonus coins together. If there is nothing to pay out, it shows "Already Claimed!".
- Money Slimes first appear in [level 3](/help/levels/level-3/) and show up in about 5 to 10% of spawns in later levels.
- They also appear in [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/) (10% of spawns), where they behave as ordinary good slimes.

## Is it worth the tap?

Five coins per slime is small next to the completion reward, but a level with many Money Slimes adds up. Over 50 levels it is a real boost toward Store pets. Never trade a time-critical tap for a Money Slime on a tight level.
