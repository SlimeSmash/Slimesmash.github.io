The Trap Slime looks harmless but punishes the tap. Popping it takes **1 point** and cuts the score you earn by **half for 2 seconds**.

## How it works

- Appears in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) from [level 18](/help/levels/level-18/).
- If you leave it alone it expires after 4 seconds with no penalty.
- The halving only affects points you gain during the 2 seconds. It does not remove points you already have.
- A [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) shield stops time and heart penalties but does **not** stop the Trap score effect.
- [Purify](/help/pets/purify/) converts Trap Slimes into Normal Slimes worth 2 points.

## Tips

- Never tap a Trap Slime in the middle of a [Mega Combo](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/) window.
- Trap Slimes in an explosion's radius are popped too, so mind the placement.
