The Mirror Slime flips the slime area horizontally. Slimes that were on the left jump to the right and the other way around.

## How it works

- The mirror lasts **5 seconds**. Popping another Mirror Slime during it extends it by 2 seconds.
- Your taps still work on whatever you see, but your muscle memory about where slimes were is wrong for a moment.
- About 8% of [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/) spawns.

## Tips

- Do not plan taps ahead of a flip. Look first, then tap.
- Stay calm. The flip does not change timing or lifetimes.
