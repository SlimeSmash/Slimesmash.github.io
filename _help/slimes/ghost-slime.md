The Ghost Slime fades in and out continuously, and you can only pop it while it is solid enough.

## How it works

- Its opacity cycles between about **10% and 100%** roughly once per second.
- A tap while the slime is below **50% opacity** is rejected ("TOO FADED!"). It stays on screen and you can try again.
- It is visible enough to hit about 60% of the time, so patience beats speed.
- It is 10% of [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/) spawns and 15% of [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/) spawns.

## Tips

- Time the tap to the moment it brightens rather than tapping repeatedly.
- In Defense Mode, treat Ghost Slimes as early threats. They are slow to deal with.
