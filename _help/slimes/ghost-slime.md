The Ghost Slime tests player timing and precision in Waves Mode.

### Mechanics
- **Effect**: Continuously ping-pongs its opacity (alpha) between 1.0 (fully visible) and 0.0 (invisible).
- **Hit Condition**: Must be at least 50% visible (opacity of 0.5 or more) to register a hit.
