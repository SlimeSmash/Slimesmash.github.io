The Normal Slime is the baseline every other slime is measured against. It pops with a single tap and never hurts you.

## How it works

- **Adventure Mode:** each Normal Slime is worth **1 point**. It stays on screen for **5 seconds**, then disappears. If you miss it, you only lose the chance to score; nothing else happens.
- **Casual Mode:** it is the most common slime (50% of spawns). Points are not used in Casual Mode. If one expires before you pop it, you lose a heart.
- **Defense Mode:** it is 40% of spawns. It marches toward your base. If it arrives it breaks your base shield.
- **Surf levels:** it slides in from the right. If it leaves the left side of the screen you lose 3 seconds and your combo breaks.

## Where else you will see it

Normal Slimes also appear as other slimes' by-products. A [Split Slime](/help/slimes/split-slime/) bursts into four tiny Normal Slimes, and [Purify](/help/pets/purify/) turns bad slimes into Normal Slimes. A popped [Shield Slime](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) turns into a Normal-style slime that is worth 2 points.

## Spawn odds by mode

| Mode | Chance per spawn |

| Casual | 50% |

## Tips

- Never ignore Normal Slimes in favour of a flashy one. Most of your score comes from them.
- Tap the slime nearest the end of its 5-second life first.
