SlimeSmash has **15 active slime types**. Which ones you meet depends on the mode. Each type is either **good** (safe to pop) or **bad** (tapping it hurts).

## Good slimes and bad slimes

A **bad slime** is one that punishes you for tapping it. Only [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) and [Trap](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) are bad. Leaving them alone costs nothing. Everything else is good, but in Casual Mode a good slime that expires costs you a heart, and in Defense Mode a slime that reaches your base breaks its shield.

## Retired slimes

[Freeze Slime](/help/slimes/freeze-slime/) (retired in v1.2.0) and [Devourer](/help/slimes/devourer/) (removed) no longer appear.
