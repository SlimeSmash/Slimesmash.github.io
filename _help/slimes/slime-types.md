Slimes are what you pop in SlimeSmash. Each slime type possesses unique behaviors, effects, and mode availability.

## Good to know

- You start with a roster of slimes and discover more as you play. The **Slime Discovery panel** tracks them.
- All slime types were made 15% larger in v1.1.7.
- Bad slimes (such as Poison and Trap slimes) are harmful to tap, but certain pet abilities (like [Maximus](/help/pets/maximus/)) can eliminate them.

## Roster Summary

### Dual Mode Slimes (Adventure & Waves)
- **[Normal Slime](/help/slimes/normal-slime/)**: Standard slime worth base score.
- **[Shield Slime](/help/slimes/shield-slime/)**: Requires a double-tap to pop. Behaviour was corrected in v1.1.5.

### Adventure Mode Slimes
- **[Explosion Slime](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/)**: Explodes nearby slimes. Worth 3 points since v1.1.5.
- **[Chain-Reaction Slime](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/)**: Pops every good slime on the screen. Added in v1.2.0.
- **[Split Slime](/help/slimes/split-slime/)**: Splits into 4 new Normal slimes when popped.
- **[Poison Slime](/help/slimes/poison-slime/)**: Bad slime that deducts 3 seconds from the clock.
- **[Trap Slime](/help/slimes/trap-slime/)**: Bad slime that applies a 0.8x score multiplier for 3 seconds.
- **[Mega Combo Slime](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/)**: Applies a 1.2x score multiplier for 3 seconds.
- **[Extra Time Slime](/help/slimes/extra-time-slime/)**: Adds 2 extra seconds to the timer.
- **[Money Slime](/help/slimes/money-slime/)**: Grants extra coins.

### Waves Mode Slimes
- **[Heart Slime](/help/slimes/heart-slime/)**: Grants an extra heart.
- **[Slow-Mo Slime](/help/slimes/slow-mo-slime/)**: Freezes the action for 2 seconds; spawning pauses while it lasts.
- **[Ghost Slime](/help/slimes/ghost-slime/)**: Fades in and out (alpha 0 to 1); requires at least 50% opacity to hit.
- **[Blind Slime](/help/slimes/blind-slime/)**: Toggles a flashlight visual impairment on/off.
- **[Mirror Slime](/help/slimes/mirror-slime/)**: Mirrors the X-coordinate for tapping controls. Behaviour was fixed in v1.1.10.

### Retired Slimes
- **[Freeze Slime](/help/slimes/freeze-slime/)**: Retired in v1.2.0 to make way for the Chain-Reaction Slime. Behaviour last fixed in v1.1.10.
- **[Devourer](/help/slimes/devourer/)**: Enemy slime type in Adventure Mode. Spawning bug fixed in v1.1.1.
