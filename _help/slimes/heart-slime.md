The Heart Slime is the rare lifeline of [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/). Popping it restores **one heart**, up to the maximum of three.

## How it works

- Appears only in Casual Mode, at about **3%** of spawns.
- If you are already at three hearts, nothing happens.
- Like every good slime, letting it expire costs you a heart, so a missed Heart Slime hurts twice.

## Tips

- If you are on one heart, a Heart Slime is the top priority on screen.
- Do not let a screen full of other slimes distract you from a heart when you have lost one.
