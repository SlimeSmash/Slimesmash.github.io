The Split Slime is worth four slimes. It scores nothing itself, but popping it releases **four tiny Normal Slimes**, each worth 1 point.

## How it works

- Appears in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) from [level 26](/help/levels/level-26/).
- The new slimes burst out in four directions and are extra spawns: they ignore the level's maximum slime count.
- Each new slime has its own 5-second lifetime.
- Pop it early. If you wait until the last second, the tiny slimes may expire before you reach them.

## Tips

- Pair it with an [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) slime. The four tiny slimes land close together.
- With [Echo](/help/pets/echo/) or a [Chain-Reaction](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/) link, the bursts are easy to clear in one move.
