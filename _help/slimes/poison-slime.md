The Poison Slime is the first **bad slime** you meet. Tapping it drains **3 seconds** from the level timer. Leave it alone and it simply fades after 4 seconds with no penalty.

## How it works

- Appears in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) from [level 2](/help/levels/level-2/).
- Worth no points. The only thing it does is take time away.
- Bad slimes never cost you anything for *expiring*. The penalty only applies if you tap one.
- A [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) shield blocks the time loss completely while it is active.
- [Purify](/help/pets/purify/) turns Poison Slimes into Normal Slimes worth 2 points each.

## Danger moments

- **Explosions:** an [Explosive Slime](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) pops every slime in range, including Poison Slimes, and the time penalty applies.
- **[Echo](/help/pets/echo/):** the second pop goes to the *nearest* slime, even a bad one.
- **[Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/):** its explosion also hits bad slimes nearby.

## Tips

- Pop a Poison Slime on purpose only when you hold a Guard shield.
- In a crowded screen, check the colour before every tap.
