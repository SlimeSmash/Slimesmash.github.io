The Extra Time Slime is a lifesaver in timed levels. Tapping it adds **2 seconds** to the clock.

## How it works

- Appears in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) from [level 4](/help/levels/level-4/).
- Two seconds is worth roughly two to three extra spawns on most levels, so it is almost always worth the tap.
- One Extra Time Slime does not fully cancel one Poison Slime: Extra Time gives 2 seconds, Poison takes 3.
- Not used in Casual or Defense Mode, which have no timer.

## Tips

- On later levels the target score is higher than simple popping provides. Extra Time Slimes are part of how you close that gap.
- On Surf levels, grab them before they leave the screen. A good slime that escapes costs you 3 seconds.
