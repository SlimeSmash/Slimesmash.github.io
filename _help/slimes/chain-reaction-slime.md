The Chain-Reaction Slime replaced the Freeze Slime in v1.2.0. It works in two steps.

## How it works

1. **Link:** pop the Chain-Reaction Slime. Every good slime on screen gets a pulsing gold outline and becomes *linked*.
2. **Detonate:** pop any linked slime. Every linked slime pops at once.

Each slime in the chain scores its own points, so a full board is a huge payoff.

- Appears in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) from [level 42](/help/levels/level-42/). It is 10% of [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/) spawns.
- The Chain-Reaction Slime itself is worth 1 point and has a short 4-second life.
- Bad slimes are not linked, so you are safe from them.

## Tips

- After linking, let the board fill with new slimes for a moment before you detonate, but not so long that linked slimes expire.
- Combine with [Mega Combo](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/): link, trigger the multiplier, then detonate.
- In Defense Mode, detonate when a wave is bunched near your base.
