The Freeze Slime was an Adventure Mode slime that froze play for a short time. It was officially **retired in v1.2.0** to make room for the [Chain-Reaction Slime](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/).

Its behaviour was last fixed in v1.1.10. It is not part of the current slime list and does not appear in any level.

For the equivalent effect, the pet [Frost](/help/pets/frost/) freezes all slimes for 3 seconds on demand.
