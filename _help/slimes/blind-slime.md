The Blind Slime messes with the lighting. Popping it fades the screen to dark for a few seconds so you cannot see the other slimes.

## How it works

- The effect fades in over about 1 second, holds for 1 second, and fades out over 1 second. Popping another Blind Slime during the hold extends it by 1 second.
- Slimes keep moving and timing out while the screen is dark.
- It is a good slime: popping it is not a mistake, but you pay the price in visibility.
- About 7% of [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/) spawns.

## Tips

- Pop other slimes first if you can, then the Blind Slime.
- Remember the positions of nearby slimes before the screen fades.
