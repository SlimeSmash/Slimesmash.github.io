The Slow-Mo Slime gives you a breather in [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/).

## How it works

- Popping it triggers a **2-second** slow-motion effect. Hitting a second one while active extends it by 2 more seconds.
- While the effect runs, spawning and slime timers are paused, so nothing expires. Taps on slimes are ignored during the pause.
- About 7% of Casual spawns.

## Tips

- Use the pause to plan the next few taps rather than to rush.
- It is more valuable at high waves, where slime counts are large.
