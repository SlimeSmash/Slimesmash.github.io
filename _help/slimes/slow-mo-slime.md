The Slow-Mo Slime gives you a short breather in Waves (Casual) Mode.

### Mechanics
- **Effect**: Freezes the action for 2 seconds. New slimes stop appearing while it lasts.
- **Stacking**: Popping another one while the effect is active adds 2 more seconds.
