The Explosion Slime (the in-game name is **Explosive**) is the main way to score in bursts. Popping it is worth **3 points** and sets off a blast that pops every slime nearby.

## How it works

- The blast radius is about 250 pixels on the game's reference canvas, roughly a fifth of the screen width on a phone held upright.
- Every slime inside the radius is popped and scores its own points. A cluster of Normal Slimes next to an Explosive Slime is a jackpot.
- The blast does **not** check whether a slime is good or bad. Poison and Trap slimes in range are popped too, and their penalties apply.
- First appears in [level 5](/help/levels/level-5/), which is also the first Surf level.
- In [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/) it is 7% of spawns and clears slimes marching toward your base.

## Tips

- Wait until the Explosive Slime is surrounded by good slimes, but not so long that it expires (5 seconds).
- Check for bad slimes inside the circle before you tap.
