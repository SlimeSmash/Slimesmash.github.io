The Shield Slime has a protective layer. The first tap breaks the shield, and the second tap pops it.

## How it works

- **First tap:** removes the shield. No points yet.
- **Second tap:** pops the slime and scores **2 points** in Adventure Mode. That is the same rate as two Normal Slimes.
- The slime's 5-second lifetime keeps running between the taps.
- First appears in [level 10](/help/levels/level-10/). It is 18% of Defense spawns and 15% of Casual spawns.
- In [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/), a Shield Slime takes two taps while marching, so start on it early.

## Tips

- Tap a Shield Slime as soon as you see it so the second tap is not rushed.
- If you have [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) active, the bigger target makes the double-tap easier.
