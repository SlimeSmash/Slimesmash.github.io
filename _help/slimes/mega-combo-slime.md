The Mega Combo Slime is the score frenzy slime. For **3 seconds** after you pop it, every point you earn is **doubled**.

## How it works

- Appears in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) from [level 34](/help/levels/level-34/).
- Scores nothing on its own. The value is in what you pop next.
- The multiplier shows next to your score while it is active.
- It does not change your combo counter, which is a separate visual meter.

## Tips

- Save your big pops. If a crowd of slimes, a Shield Slime or an Explosive Slime is available, tap the Mega Combo Slime first and then go for them.
- Avoid [Trap Slimes](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) during the window. Their half-score effect fights your doubling.
- Late levels have goals that cannot be reached without at least a few well-timed Mega Combo windows.
