SlimeSmash saves your levels, coins, pets and achievements so you can pick up where you left off.

## How saving works

- **Cloud save.** Progress is saved to the cloud so it can sync across your devices. This is why the game uses an online player identity.
- **Offline play.** If your connection drops, the game keeps your progress and syncs it when you are back online.
- **Two devices, one account.** If both devices changed your progress, the game reconciles the two saves rather than overwriting one.
- **Encryption.** Saves have been encrypted since version 1.1.1. Progress was reset once as part of that upgrade.

## Protecting your progress

- Keep the game updated. Earlier versions had save bugs that were fixed in v1.0.2 and v1.0.10.
- Connect to the internet now and then so your progress reaches the cloud.
- Uninstalling without cloud sync, or wiping your device, can lose progress. The [Terms](/terms.html) cover this.

<div class="note" markdown="1">
**Deleting your account is permanent.** Progress, coins and achievements cannot be restored. See [Delete your account](/help/guides/delete-account/).
</div>
