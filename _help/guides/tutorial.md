New players get a short guided intro.

## Intro

1. The game points you to the Store and asks you to choose your first pet.
2. You equip it and start level 1.

## Level 1

- Tap the green slimes to pop them.
- Use the pet button to try your pet's ability.
- The level starts without the usual 3-2-1 countdown.
- Level 1 no longer shows the welcome panel when replayed (fixed in the next update).

## Slime Discovery

The first time you meet a new slime type, the **Slime Discovery** panel explains it. The number of slimes you have discovered is also shown if you ever need to resolve a [save conflict](/help/guides/progress-and-saving/).

See [Getting started](/help/guides/getting-started/).
