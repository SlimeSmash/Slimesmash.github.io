## Sources of coins

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Adventure level completion | 10 to 203+ per level, 10,752 total for all 50 |
| [Money slime](/help/slimes/money-slime/) | 5 per slime (paid on win) |
| [Flash](/help/pets/flash/) fast pop | 5 per pop |
| Casual wave | 10 per wave |
| Casual record | +100 bonus |
| Defense wave | 15 per wave |
| Defense record | +100 bonus |
| Daily reward | 250, 300, 400, 500, 700 (5-day cycle, 2,150 total) |
| Daily missions | Varies by rarity, plus a bonus for finishing all three |

Coins are capped at 99,999,999.

## Best ways to farm

1. **Defense Mode pays the most per wave** (15 vs 10). Beating a checkpoint wave makes later runs shorter.
2. **Claim the daily reward every day.** A full 5-day cycle is worth 2,150 coins.
3. **Complete all three daily missions.** See [Daily rewards and missions](/help/guides/daily-rewards-and-missions/).
4. **Replay Adventure levels you can win easily.** The reward is paid when you win.
5. **Go for record waves.** Each new record in Casual or Defense is a flat 100 coins.

Coins have no real-world value. See [Coins and the Store](/help/guides/coins-and-store/).
