Your base survives **one** hit, then it is destroyed. Slimes march from all directions at a constant speed, so waves are about *volume*, not speed.

## Key facts

- Slimes never expire. Every slime must be popped.
- Wave 5, 10, 15 and so on are **Surge** waves with 1.5× slimes at double spawn speed.
- You save a **checkpoint** every 5 waves.

See the [Defense wave table](/help/reference/defense-wave-table/).

## Strategy

1. **Pop from the outside in.** Slimes far from the base are free to ignore for a moment, but never let one get close.
2. **Save Explosive and Chain-Reaction slimes for bunching.** Slimes converge toward the middle, so detonate when they are 2 or 3 deep.
3. **Shield slimes first.** They need two taps.
4. **Ghost slimes only take hits when visible.** Do not waste taps.
5. **Prepare for Surge waves.** Use [Frost](/help/pets/frost/), [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) or [Vortex](/help/pets/vortex/) when the label turns red.

## After a hit

You have 1.5 seconds of invulnerability. Use it to clear the closest slimes.

## Checkpoints

Beat wave 5 and your next run starts at wave 6. You can stack that progress safely. See [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/).
