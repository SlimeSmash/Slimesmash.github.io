SlimeSmash can be played on Windows through Google Play Games. Version 1.1.10, the PC Support Update, added keyboard support.

## Keyboard controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> / Back | Go back |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Activate your pet's ability during a level |

Some features that are not supported on PC are turned off for compatibility.

## More PC notes

- Vibration is hidden on PC because it does nothing.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> pauses in [Defense Mode](/help/modes/defense-mode/) and other modes.
- Use the mouse like a finger: click slimes to pop them.
- High FPS in [Settings](/help/guides/settings/) uses your display's refresh rate when it is 120 Hz or higher.
