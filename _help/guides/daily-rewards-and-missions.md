## Daily rewards

Open the **Daily Reward** button on the main menu once a day and claim.

| Day | Coins |
|---|---|
| 1 | 250 |
| 2 | 300 |
| 3 | 400 |
| 4 | 500 |
| 5 | 700 |

- A full cycle is worth **2,150 coins**, then the cycle starts again.
- You can claim once every 24 hours.
- If you wait **more than 48 hours** since your last claim, your streak resets to day 1.
- The game can send a notification to tell you a reward is ready.

## Daily missions

- You get **3 missions per day**. They reset at midnight UTC.
- Mission types: play Adventure levels, win Adventure levels, earn coins, pop slimes, and play for a number of minutes.
- Missions have a rarity: **Common** (base reward), **Rare** (1.8× reward) and **Epic** (3× reward).
- Finish all three to open the **Grand Chest** and earn a 500-coin bonus.
- You can watch a rewarded ad to **reroll** a mission you dislike (limited per day) or to boost a reward. Ads for missions are always optional.
- After every run the game shows your mission progress.

The daily mission streak is removed in the next update, and a mission rework is planned. See [Upcoming update](/help/updates/upcoming-update/).

## Notifications

Notifications are optional. To turn them off, use your phone's notification settings for SlimeSmash (Android Settings, Apps, SlimeSmash, Notifications).

If rewards or notifications are not arriving, see [Troubleshooting](/help/guides/troubleshooting/).
