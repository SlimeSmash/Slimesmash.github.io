## Daily rewards

Log in on consecutive days to build a streak and earn bonuses. The game can send a push notification to remind you when a reward is ready.

## Missions

- Missions give gold coins when you complete them.
- Daily missions reset every day.
- After every run, the game shows your mission progress.

## Notifications

Notifications are optional. To turn them off, use your phone's notification settings for SlimeSmash (Android Settings, Apps, SlimeSmash, Notifications).

If rewards or notifications are not arriving, see [Troubleshooting](/help/guides/troubleshooting/).
