## Your identity

When you first open the game it creates an online player identity for you automatically. That is what lets your progress sync to the cloud. See [Progress and saving](/help/guides/progress-and-saving/).

## Google Play Games

Signing in to Google Play Games lets you:

- Appear on the [leaderboards](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/).
- Earn [achievements](/help/reference/achievements/).
- Move to a new device and keep your progress.

Switching your Google Play Games profile no longer creates a duplicate identity (fixed in 2.0). Signing in with Google after launching offline also works now.

## When two saves disagree

If the save on your device and the cloud save differ, the game asks which to keep and shows each one's **level**, **coins** and **slimes discovered**. Choose carefully: the other save is replaced. See [Progress and saving](/help/guides/progress-and-saving/).
