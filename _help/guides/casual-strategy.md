Casual Mode is endless. The numbers grow every wave, so your goal is to keep three hearts for as long as possible.

## The numbers

- Wave 10 has 22 slimes, wave 20 has 42, wave 30 has 62.
- The spawn delay shrinks until wave 36, when it reaches 0.15 s.
- Each good slime that expires costs a heart.

See the [Casual wave table](/help/reference/casual-wave-table/).

## Strategy

1. **Clear the oldest slime first.** Every slime lives 5 seconds.
2. **Priority list:** [Heart](/help/slimes/heart-slime/) (when hurt) → [Shield](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) (needs two taps, start early) → everything else.
3. **Treat [Ghost](/help/slimes/ghost-slime/) as a timing puzzle.** Tap when it is bright.
4. **Mirror and Blind slimes are a nuisance, not a danger.** Pop them last, or when the screen is quiet.
5. **Use [Slow-Mo](/help/slimes/slow-mo-slime/) to reset** when too many slimes are on screen.

## Pets

- [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) every 16 seconds gives you free breathing room.
- [Vortex](/help/pets/vortex/) is a screen wipe every 29 seconds.
- [Bones](/help/pets/bones/) rescues 30% of expiring slimes.

See [Pets in endless modes](/help/reference/pets-in-endless-modes/).
