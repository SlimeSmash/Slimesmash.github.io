SlimeSmash is free and shows ads. When available in your version, a one-time **Remove Ads** purchase through Google Play turns off the ads that appear between games.

- It is a single purchase, not a subscription.
- Optional rewarded ads (for missions) are still your choice.
- If you have bought it and ads still show, update the game, restart it, and then [contact support](/help/guides/contact-support/) with your Google Play order details.

See [Ads and privacy](/help/guides/ads-and-privacy/).
