| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sound | Turns sound effects on or off |
| Vibration | Turns haptic feedback on or off (hidden on PC) |
| High FPS | The game runs at 60 FPS by default. High FPS uses 120 FPS on devices with a 120 Hz screen |
| Language | English, Español, Français, Deutsch or Português (BR). See [Languages](/help/guides/languages/) |
| Google Play Games | Sign in or link your account. See [Accounts and sign-in](/help/guides/accounts-and-sign-in/) |
| Player name and icon | See [Player name and profile](/help/guides/player-name-and-profile/) |
| Delete account | See [Delete your account](/help/guides/delete-account/) |
| Player ID | Your unique player ID, useful when you [contact support](/help/guides/contact-support/) |
| Privacy options | Review your consent choices for ads and analytics. See [Ads and privacy](/help/guides/ads-and-privacy/) |

## Notes

- There is no music. The game has sound effects only (music was removed in 2.0), and turning Sound off silences everything, including pop sounds.
- High FPS uses more battery on 120 Hz screens, which is why 60 FPS is the default.
- Settings are saved on your device.
