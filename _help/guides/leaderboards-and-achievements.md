## Leaderboards

SlimeSmash uses Unity Leaderboards for cross-platform global rankings where you can track your wave clearance totals and see how high you rank worldwide.

- Leaderboards require an active internet connection.
- Android players can also connect a Google Play Games account for platform-specific leaderboards and achievements.
- Your display name is filtered for inappropriate words.
- The icon you pick in the [Store](/help/guides/coins-and-store/) appears next to your name.

## Achievements

Achievements are unlocked through Google Play Games on Android devices. Version 1.1.1 added 10 new ones.

## Fair play

Cheats, memory editors, bots and exploits are not allowed. Accounts that abuse the game can be suspended. See the [Terms](/terms.html).
