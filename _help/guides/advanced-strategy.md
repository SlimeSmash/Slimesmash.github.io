From around level 26 the target score is higher than what you get by popping each good slime once. You need bonuses. This is how.

## Build your multiplier windows

A [Mega Combo](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/) slime doubles score for 3 seconds. Do not tap it the moment it appears. Wait until there is a pack of slimes, an [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) or a [Shield](/help/slimes/shield-slime/) slime on screen, tap it, then cash in.

## Chain-Reaction timing

A [Chain-Reaction](/help/slimes/chain-reaction-slime/) slime links all good slimes on screen. You then pop *any* linked slime to detonate them all. The best moment is just before linked slimes expire. Combine with Mega Combo for the biggest single payoff.

## Avoid the double penalty

- A Trap slime costs 1 point and halves your gains for 2 seconds. Never tap one during a Mega Combo window.
- Explosions and [Echo](/help/pets/echo/) can hit bad slimes. Check the area before tapping.

## Pet combos

| Combo | Why it works |
|---|---|
| [Lure](/help/pets/lure/) + Explosive slime | Lure bunches slimes into the blast |
| [Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/) + Split slime | Every 5th pop sets off a blast on the four tiny slimes |
| [Purify](/help/pets/purify/) + Mega Combo | Converted slimes are worth 2 points, doubled to 4 |
| [Zap](/help/pets/zap/) + anything | Free pops while you work on the rest |

## Read the level

Open the level page and check the **slime mix** and **difficulty rating**. For example [level 42](/help/levels/level-42/) introduces Chain-Reaction. Levels rated "Needs bonuses" cannot be cleared without chain pops, extra time or multipliers.

See [Score and multipliers](/help/reference/score-and-multipliers/) for the exact numbers.
