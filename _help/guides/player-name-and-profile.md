## Name rules

- **3 to 12 characters.**
- Letters (including accented letters), numbers and spaces.
- You can change your name once every **60 seconds**.
- Names are checked by a profanity filter that also catches common letter swaps such as 4 for A.

New players start with a randomly generated name that you can change.

## Icons

The icon you pick appears next to your name on the leaderboard. **Default** is free. **Star**, **Heart** and **Lucky** cost 100 coins each in the [Store](/help/guides/coins-and-store/).
