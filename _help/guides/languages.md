SlimeSmash is available in:

- English
- Español (Spanish)
- Français (French)
- Deutsch (German)
- Português (Brazil)

Change the language in [Settings](/help/guides/settings/). Names of every slime and pet in each language are on [Names in other languages](/help/reference/localized-names/).

This wiki is in English only. Arabic and other languages are not supported at the moment.
