SlimeSmash is available in:

- English
- Español (Spanish)
- Français (French)
- Deutsch (German)
- Português (Brazil)

Everything in the game is translated: menus, the tutorial, messages, errors and Google sign-in. The pixel font draws accented letters and punctuation, and [player names](/help/guides/player-name-and-profile/) can include them too.

Change the language in [Settings](/help/guides/settings/). Names of every slime and pet in each language are on [Names in other languages](/help/reference/localized-names/).

This wiki is in English only. Arabic is not available, because it needs right-to-left text and an Arabic font.
