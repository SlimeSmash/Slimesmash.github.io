## The basics

1. **Tap the slime nearest to expiring first.** Normal slimes last 5 seconds. Bad ones last 4.
2. **Never tap purple or toxic slimes** on purpose. [Poison](/help/slimes/poison-slime/) costs 3 seconds.
3. **Always take [Extra Time](/help/slimes/extra-time-slime/) slimes.** Two seconds buys about two more spawns.
4. **Use explosions on crowds.** A blast from an [Explosive](/help/slimes/explosion-slime/) slime pops every slime in range, and each scores.
5. **Keep your finger moving.** Tapping fast matters more than aiming perfectly, because slimes are generous targets.

## Pets and coins

6. **Equip a pet before every level.** Start with [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/), then switch to [Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/) at level 4.
7. **Save for Frost or Echo first** (800 coins each). See [Which pet should I get?](/help/pets/choosing-a-pet/).
8. **Claim your daily reward and missions every day.** That is about 430 coins a day on average from the reward cycle alone.
9. **Replay early levels for coins** if you are short. Winning pays the completion reward. See [Earning coins fast](/help/guides/earning-coins-fast/).

## Habits to avoid

10. **Do not chase one slime.** If it is far away, take two close ones instead.
11. **Do not tap bad slimes in a rush.** On a crowded screen, look before you tap.
12. **Do not hold your pet ability for too long.** Three uses per level is generous, so spend them.
