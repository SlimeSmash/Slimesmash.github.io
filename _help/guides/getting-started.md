SlimeSmash is a fast-paced pixel arcade wave-survival game for Android. You pop slimes, collect gold coins, and unlock companion and pets that change how you play.

## Your first minutes

1. **Install the game** from [Google Play]({{ site.data.site.play_url }}). It is free to download.
2. **Pick a mode.** [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) has 50+ handcrafted levels. [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/) is endless waves at your own pace.
3. **Pop slimes and collect coins.** Coins are earned in-game and spent in the [Store](/help/guides/coins-and-store/).
4. **Use your starter pet.** [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) is a free starter companion.
5. **Connect Google Play Games** to appear on the [leaderboards](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/) and earn achievements.
6. **Come back daily.** [Daily rewards and missions](/help/guides/daily-rewards-and-missions/) give extra coins.

## Good to know

- Your progress is saved to the cloud so it can sync across devices. See [Progress and saving](/help/guides/progress-and-saving/).
- The game shows ads. See [Ads and privacy](/help/guides/ads-and-privacy/).
- Playing on a computer? See [Playing on PC](/help/guides/playing-on-pc/).
