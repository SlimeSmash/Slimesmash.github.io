SlimeSmash is a fast-paced pixel arcade game for Android. You pop slimes, collect gold coins, and unlock pets that change how you play.

## Your first minutes

1. **Install the game** from [Google Play]({{ site.data.site.play_url }}). It is free to download.
2. **Follow the tutorial.** It walks you through choosing your first pet in the Store, then your first level. See [The tutorial](/help/guides/tutorial/).
3. **Play Adventure Mode.** [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) has 50 levels. The first levels are slow and forgiving.
4. **Pop green slimes, avoid purple ones.** Bad slimes cost you time or points. See [Slime types](/help/slimes/slime-types/).
5. **Use your pet.** [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) is free. Tap the pet button, or press <kbd>Space</kbd> on PC.
6. **Claim your daily reward** from the main menu. See [Daily rewards and missions](/help/guides/daily-rewards-and-missions/).
7. **Connect Google Play Games** to appear on [leaderboards](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/) and earn [achievements](/help/reference/achievements/).

## What to aim for

| Goal | When |
|---|---|
| Clear level 4 | Unlocks [Casual Mode](/help/modes/casual-mode/) (reach level 5) |
| Clear level 3 | Unlocks the pet [Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/) |
| Save 800 coins | Buy your first Store pet. See [Which pet?](/help/pets/choosing-a-pet/) |
| Clear level 50 | Finish Adventure Mode |
