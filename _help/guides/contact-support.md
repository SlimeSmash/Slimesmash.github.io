Email **[{{ site.data.site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.site.email }})**. SlimeSmash is a solo indie game, so replies may take a little while.

## What to include

- Your device model and Android version.
- The SlimeSmash version you are playing.
- What happened, and the steps that led to it.
- A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

## Reporting a level

Adventure Mode has a report system inside the game for levels that look broken or unfair.

## Feedback

Ideas and feedback are welcome at the same address.
