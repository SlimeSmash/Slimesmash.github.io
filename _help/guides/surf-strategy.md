Surf levels are every 5th Adventure level. Slimes slide in from the right and never expire, but a good slime that leaves the left edge costs **3 seconds** and breaks your combo.

## Rules to remember

- Speed rises by up to **35%** as the timer runs down. The last 10 seconds are the fastest.
- Bad slimes can leave safely. You only lose time on *good* slimes.
- [Bones](/help/pets/bones/) does nothing here.

## Best approach

1. **Tap slimes at the right of the screen as they enter.** You have the most time then.
2. **Do not chase.** Move to where the slime is going, not where it is.
3. **Equip [Guard](/help/pets/guard/).** It blocks the 3-second miss penalty. Use it when the pace picks up.
4. **[Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/)** makes slimes bigger. This helps because the slimes move.
5. **[Lure](/help/pets/lure/)** pulls slimes toward your last tap, which helps with wave motion.
