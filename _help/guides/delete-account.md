You can delete your account and data in the game or by email.

## In the game

Go to **Settings, then Delete Account**.

## By email

Email [{{ site.data.site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.site.email }}) with **Data Deletion Request** in the subject line.

We process deletion requests in a timely manner, typically within 30 days. We delete the SlimeSmash data we control, except data that third-party providers must keep or that the law requires us to keep.

<div class="note" markdown="1">
**This is permanent.** Progress, coins and achievements cannot come back afterwards.
</div>

Read the full [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html#deletion-request).
