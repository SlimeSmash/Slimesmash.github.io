Gold coins are the in-game currency. You earn them by playing and spend them in the Store.

## Earning coins

See [Earning coins fast](/help/guides/earning-coins-fast/) for every source.

## What the Store sells

| Item | Rarity | Price |
|---|---|---|
| [Echo](/help/pets/echo/) | Rare | 800 |
| [Frost](/help/pets/frost/) | Rare | 800 |
| [Bones](/help/pets/bones/) | Epic | 1,200 |
| [Flash](/help/pets/flash/) | Epic | 1,500 |
| [Zap](/help/pets/zap/) | Epic | 1,500 |
| [Vortex](/help/pets/vortex/) | Mythic | 2,000 |
| Star, Heart and Lucky icons | Common | 100 each |

Pets earned by reaching Adventure levels ([Rumble](/help/pets/rumble/), [Guard](/help/pets/guard/), [Lure](/help/pets/lure/), [Purify](/help/pets/purify/)) and your starter [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) are not sold in the Store.

Items are sorted from rarest to most common. Temporary sales can appear, and prices can change in updates.

## Rarity colours

Common is green, Rare is blue, Epic is purple, Mythic is red and Legendary is yellow.

## Player icons

The Store has an Icons tab (added in v1.1.7). Your icon is shown next to your name on the leaderboard. The Default icon is free.

## Good to know

- Coins are earned entirely in-game. They have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged or transferred.
- There are no real-money coin packs.
- Pet upgrades were removed in 2.0. See [What's new in 2.0](/help/updates/whats-new-in-2-0/).
- The Store has a **Skins** tab that says "Coming Soon!". It is not active yet.
