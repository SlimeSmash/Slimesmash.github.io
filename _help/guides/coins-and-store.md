Gold coins are the in-game currency. You earn them by playing, and you spend them in the Store.

## Earning coins

- Playing levels in [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/).
- Completing missions and claiming [daily rewards](/help/guides/daily-rewards-and-missions/).

## Spending coins

- **Unloack rare pets.** See [Companions and pets](/help/pets/companions-and-pets/).
- **Upgrade pets stats** to boost your survival power.
- **Player icons.** The Store has an Icons tab (added in v1.1.7). Your icon is shown on the global leaderboard.

Coins are earned entirely in-game. They have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged or transferred between players.
