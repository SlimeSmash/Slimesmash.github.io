SlimeSmash is free to download and shows ads through Google AdMob to support development.

- Where required by law, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.
- You can reset or disable your Advertising ID in your device settings.
- Crash and gameplay analytics help fix bugs and improve the game.

Everything the game collects, and every service it uses, is listed in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html).

## How ads work

- **Interstitial ads** are full-screen ads between games. They are spaced at least 75 seconds apart.
- **Rewarded ads** are optional. You choose to watch one, for example to reroll a daily mission.
- Ads never appear in the middle of a level.
- In some regions a consent form appears the first time you play.
- A one-time purchase may be available to remove interstitial ads. See [Remove Ads](/help/guides/remove-ads/).

## Your data

You can delete your account and data at any time. See [Delete your account](/help/guides/delete-account/).
