Achievements are tracked through Google Play Games. You need to be signed in for them to unlock. See [Leaderboards and achievements](/help/guides/leaderboards-and-achievements/).

## Adventure milestones

| Achievement | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| First Steps | Clear [level 1](/help/levels/level-1/) |
| Getting the Hang | Clear [level 5](/help/levels/level-5/) |
| Adventurer | Clear [level 10](/help/levels/level-10/) |
| Slime Crusher | Clear [level 20](/help/levels/level-20/) |
| Veteran | Clear [level 35](/help/levels/level-35/) |
| Slime Legend | Clear [level 50](/help/levels/level-50/) |

## Slime-popping milestones

Four incremental achievements track your total slimes popped: **First 100 Slimes**, **Slime Hunter**, **Slime Master** and **Slime Destroyer**. Their targets are shown in Google Play Games.

Version 1.1.1 added 10 new achievements, which is this set.
