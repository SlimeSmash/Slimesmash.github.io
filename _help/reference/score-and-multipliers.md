## Score

Each popped slime adds its **base score** multiplied by your current **score multiplier**, rounded to a whole number.

| Slime | Base score |
|---|---|
| Normal | 1 |
| Explosive | 3 |
| Shield (second tap) | 2 |
| Chain-Reaction | 1 |
| Purified slime | 2 |
| Trap | −1 |
| Poison, Money, Extra Time, Split, Mega Combo | 0 |

Every slime popped by an explosion or chain adds its own score.

## Multipliers

| Source | Multiplier | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| [Mega Combo Slime](/help/slimes/mega-combo-slime/) | ×2 | 3 s |
| [Trap Slime](/help/slimes/trap-slime/) | ×0.5 | 2 s |

The current multiplier is shown by the score when it is above 1.

## Combos

The combo counter goes up with every good pop. It resets after **1.5 seconds** without one, or when you pop a bad slime or miss on a Surf level. Milestone messages appear at **5, 10, 20, 30 and 50**. The combo counter is a visual and skill meter: **it does not change your score.**

## Time changes

| Event | Change |
|---|---|
| Pop an Extra Time slime | +2 s |
| Pop a Poison slime | −3 s |
| Good slime leaves a Surf level | −3 s |
| [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) active | Time losses blocked |
| [Frost](/help/pets/frost/) active | Clock stops |

## Coins in a level

Winning pays the level's completion reward plus 5 coins per Money slime and 5 coins per fast [Flash](/help/pets/flash/) pop. See [Earning coins fast](/help/guides/earning-coins-fast/).
