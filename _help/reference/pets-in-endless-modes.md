In [Adventure Mode](/help/modes/adventure-mode/) ability pets have a **limited number of uses** per level. In [Casual](/help/modes/casual-mode/) and [Defense](/help/modes/defense-mode/) modes there is **no use limit**, so the cooldown is lengthened to compensate:

**Endless cooldown = base cooldown + 18 ÷ max uses** (whole number, rounded down).

| Pet | Base cooldown | Max uses | Endless cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Echo](/help/pets/echo/) | 8 s | 4 | 12 s |
| [Chubby](/help/pets/chubby/) | 10 s | 3 | 16 s |
| [Guard](/help/pets/guard/) | 10 s | 3 | 16 s |
| [Frost](/help/pets/frost/) | 12 s | 3 | 18 s |
| [Purify](/help/pets/purify/) | 15 s | 3 | 21 s |
| [Vortex](/help/pets/vortex/) | 20 s | 2 | 29 s |

Passive pets work the same in every mode. Cooldowns pause while the game is paused.
